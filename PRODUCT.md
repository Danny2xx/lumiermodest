# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women across the UK, of all ages, shopping for modest wear — everyday abayas
and hijabs through to occasion pieces for Eid, weddings and events. Most arrive
on a phone, often from Instagram, TikTok or a WhatsApp message, and many are
buying from LumierModest for the first time.

Because it's a young independent label rather than a known retailer, the job
isn't only "find a nice abaya". It's "decide I can trust this shop with my
money": that the fabric is what it claims, that it will fit, that it arrives
when promised, and that I can send it back if it doesn't work.

## Product Purpose

A direct-to-customer UK storefront for a small independent modest-fashion
label: abayas, hijabs, street wear and evening dress. The owner runs it
herself through an admin panel — adding products, photos and categories
without needing a developer.

Success is a customer who finds something she loves, buys it without friction,
trusts that it will arrive and fit, and comes back for the next drop. Not
one-off impulse buys squeezed out with urgency tactics.

## Brand Personality

Warm, personal, elegant. The voice already exists on the About page — plain
speech about a real frustration, first person plural, signed "With warmth,
LumierModest". It talks like a friend who knows fashion: unhurried, specific,
never salesy.

Emotionally it should feel like being *looked after* — a sense of occasion when
browsing, and reassurance at the moment of paying. Confidence, calm, care.
Never pressure, never grandeur for its own sake.

## Anti-references

- **Fast-fashion pressure** (Shein, Temu): countdown timers, "HURRY", stacked
  pop-ups, permanent fake sales, spinning discount wheels. Ruled out explicitly
  by the owner.
- **Cold luxury**: distant and intimidating — all-black minimalism, tiny grey
  type, prices hidden behind "enquire". Elegance here must stay welcoming.
- **Generic template shop**: the off-the-shelf theme look, where nothing on the
  page could only belong to this label.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth over pressure.** Persuade through care, clarity and good
   photography. Never urgency tricks, guilt or artificial scarcity. If a piece
   really is the last one, say so plainly, once.
2. **Promise only what's real.** Every claim — discounts, delivery times,
   returns, customer photos, "free shipping" — must be true on the day it's
   read. No placeholder text, and no offer the site can't actually honour.
3. **Answer doubts at the moment of decision.** Fit, fabric, delivery cost and
   returns belong beside the buy button, not buried in a footer page.
4. **Fewest steps from wanting to owning.** Every screen between "I want this"
   and "it's mine" must earn its place. Nothing asked twice.
5. **Recognisably hers.** Prefer details that could only belong to
   LumierModest — her words, her photography, her flourishes — over patterns
   copied from other shops.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, treated as a floor rather than an aspiration, because the
customer base spans all ages.

- Body and helper text must reach 4.5:1 contrast. Much of the current small
  grey text sits near 3.7:1 on cream and has to be darkened.
- No decorative micro-type: helper text stays legible at a glance.
- The bag, saved items, search and any modal must be operable by keyboard,
  close on Escape, trap focus while open, and stay out of the tab order when
  closed.
- Every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative, including the
  homepage hero slideshow, which should not auto-advance for those users.
- Buttons and inputs carry real labels, not icons alone.
